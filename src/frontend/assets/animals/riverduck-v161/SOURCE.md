# Ducky Pond Duck

- Source reference: Ducky by CazBee, https://caz-bee.itch.io/ducky
- License: CC0, Creative Commons Zero v1.0 Universal, as listed on the itch.io page.
- Original pack format listed by the author: PNG sprites, 48x48 dimensions, idle animation with 2 frames and walking animation with 4 frames.
- Local integration: redrawn and packaged as six 96x96 transparent pond frames so the existing pond movement, waterline crop, thumbnail, and flee interactions can remain stable.
- Credit is not required by the license, but the source is kept here for provenance.
